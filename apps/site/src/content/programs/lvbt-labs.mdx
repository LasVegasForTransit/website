A better bus route, a safer walk, or a more connected neighborhood starts with understanding what
could change. LVBT Labs builds tools that help people explore those questions and make the case for
better transportation in Southern Nevada.

## Tools for local questions

[TransitMapper](https://labs.lasvegasfortransit.org/transit-mapper/) lets you sketch routes on a
real street map, place stops and stations, and explore how a transit system could operate. It gives
a conversation about a proposed route something concrete to work with.

For [Week Without Driving](/programs/week-without-driving/), civic tech supports participation: trip
sharing, rider guides, a bus stop finder, and Digital Transit Bingo on the
[challenge site](https://lvwwd.org/). The tools serve the people taking the challenge and the
advocacy behind it.

## Build with us

Labs projects develop in public. Some test an idea; others help people understand public data or
work through a planning question. We share methods and source material where possible so people can
inspect the work and help improve it.

If you have a local question a tool could help answer, or want to contribute to a project, start
with the [contributing guide](https://github.com/LasVegasForTransit/labs/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
The [source code](https://github.com/LasVegasForTransit/labs) is open for people to explore, adapt,
and contribute to.
