[Week Without Driving](https://weekwithoutdriving.org/) is a national challenge organized by America
Walks. It invites people who usually drive to make trips another way and notice what the
transportation system asks of people who cannot drive or choose not to.

Las Vegans for Better Transit brings the challenge to Southern Nevada. We invite neighbors to try a
trip without driving, share what they experience, and listen to the people who already travel this
way every day. We hope to make this an annual part of our work.

The dates, participation details, and resources for the current edition live on our dedicated
[Week Without Driving site](https://lvwwd.org/).
