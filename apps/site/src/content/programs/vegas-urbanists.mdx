Each meetup starts with one topic about life in Southern Nevada. Why is a short walk to the store so
difficult? What could change along a bus route? What does a new development mean for the people
nearby? We talk through questions like these using places and experiences we know.

Then there’s time to catch up, share neighborhood news, and meet people. Bring a question or
something you’ve noticed on your block. People of all ages are welcome, and you can join the
discussion or just listen. No background in planning is needed.

Come back for a new topic each month and get to know the people around the table. As we follow
what’s changing in our neighborhoods, a concern about a street or an idea worth trying can become
something we work on together.
