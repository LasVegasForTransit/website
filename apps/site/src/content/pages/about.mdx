---
title: About LVBT
summary:
  We are the grassroots advocacy organization fighting for world-class public transit and supportive
  land use in the Las&nbsp;Vegas Valley.
---

For a metro of 2.4 million people, we run the largest bus network in the American West and have
almost no organized public voice defending it. In April 2026 that gap stopped being abstract. The
Regional Transportation Commission warned the State Legislature that, without dedicated funding,
about 42% of the Valley's bus service would be cut by 2028. A city that already asks too much of the
people who can't drive was about to ask far more.

On April 17, 2026, I stopped waiting for someone else to organize a response. I started Las Vegans
for Better Transit.

I want bus stops with shade. I want a kid to be able to cross town to see a friend without a parent
in the driver's seat. I want hubs where we have strip malls, and streets that invite you to stay.
None of that happens on its own, and no one else was going to build the constituency that makes
elected officials answer for it.

> A developed country is not a place where the poor have cars. It's where the rich use public
> transportation.
>
> <cite>Gustavo Petro, President of Colombia, 2018</cite>
