## Overview

College Faculty Outreach recruits UNLV, CSN, and Nevada State faculty as transit-advocacy allies,
witnesses, writers, and contributors of expertise. It connects academic knowledge to public
decisions about transit, land use, health, economics, and daily life in the Valley.

The project begins with faculty who already have a reason to care: their own commutes, their
students' commutes, their research, or the parking and access pressures on their campuses.

## Motivation

Faculty voices can connect transit to student attendance, workforce access, public health, housing
costs, campus parking, economic mobility, and regional competitiveness.

LVBT will build a practical channel for faculty who are willing to speak publicly, connect with
journalists or legislators, and contribute to research and accountability work.

## Approach

Outreach starts with departments where transit and land use already intersect with the work:
economics, real estate, urban policy, public administration, architecture, public health, social
sciences, and applied technology.

Priority starting points include UNLV's Lee Business School, College of Urban Affairs, School of
Architecture and Urban Studies, School of Public Health, CSN departments, and Nevada State faculty
as relationships develop.

## Activities

LVBT will maintain a roster of faculty allies with their areas of expertise, public-contact
preferences, and possible contributions to research, testimony, op-eds, or explainers. That roster
gives the organization a practical way to find the right expert when a hearing, reporter, or project
needs one.

People will see faculty-authored articles, quoted expert notes in LVBT reports, classroom
partnerships, and testimony from people who can connect transit to health, economics, housing,
public administration, and campus access.
