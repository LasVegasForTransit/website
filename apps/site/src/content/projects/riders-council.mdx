## Overview

The Riders' Council is a standing advisory body of regular RTC riders. It meets quarterly, reviews
LVBT positions, helps shape public asks, and testifies when members are willing.

The council is meant to include riders from across the Valley, across occupations, and across
different kinds of trips.

## Motivation

Strong transit advocacy gives riders real authority in the work. LVBT will build that authority into
the organization through a standing council of people who use the system.

The council gives riders a regular way to push back, add route-level knowledge, and put names and
lived experience behind public positions.

## Approach

LVBT will recruit through bus-stop conversations, street-interview projects, video projects, and
allied organizations. Members will receive compensation for meeting attendance and explicit
time-on-task, with transit fare covered.

The council will be the first audience for major LVBT positions before they go public. Membership
can be listed at each member's chosen level of identification.

## Activities

The Riders Council will seat a small group of regular transit riders with quarterly meetings, public
minutes, and a clear way to shape LVBT positions before they are published. Rider input will be
attributed when members want credit and protected when they do not.

The council will review major publications, identify rider priorities, prepare testimony for the
2027 session, and help LVBT avoid speaking about riders without riders in the room.
