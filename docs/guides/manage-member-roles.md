# Manage member roles

Use roles to record responsibilities such as Treasurer or Volunteer coordinator. A member can have
several roles and belong to several committees. Assign these independently.

## Create a role

1. Sign in to the staff portal as an administrator.
2. Open **Members**, then **Member records → Manage roles**. You can also select **Manage roles**
   beside the Roles section on a member's profile.
3. Enter a short, distinct **Role name**, then select **Add role**. The name is now available on
   member profiles.

## Assign or remove a role

1. Find the member and open their profile.
2. Under **Roles**, choose a role and select **Add role**. Repeat for any other responsibilities.
3. To end a responsibility, select **Remove** beside that role. Their other roles and committees
   remain as they are.

New roles can be assigned only to current members. Existing assignments remain visible when a member
leaves LVBT, and an administrator can remove them. Role names describe responsibilities; they do not
give staff-portal, Google or Discord access. Existing administrator and committee access settings
continue to control those permissions.

## Rename a role or stop offering it

- Select **Rename** beside a role, enter the new name, and select **Save name**. Existing member
  assignments show the new name.
- Select **Archive** beside a role and confirm **Archive role**. It disappears from new assignment
  choices. Members who already have it keep it, marked **Archived**, until you remove it from their
  profiles.

If a form expires, reload the current page before trying again. If a name is already in use, choose
a distinct name. Staff actions record who changed the role or assignment; you do not need to enter
an extra note.
