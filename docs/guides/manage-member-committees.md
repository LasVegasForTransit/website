# Manage a person's committees

Committees show where someone participates. A person can belong to several committees. Manage
organization-wide responsibilities separately in [member roles](manage-member-roles.md).

## Before you start

Sign in to the intended staff portal with your linked LVBT Google account and current membership.
You need access to manage the selected person's committee. Use test people in preview.

## Add a committee

1. Open **Members**, find the person and open their profile.
2. Under **Committees**, choose an available committee.
3. Select **Add to committee**. Confirm the committee appears in their list.

Adding a committee does not assign an organization role or committee-lead access. A committee
already on the person's list is not offered again. You can add committees only for current LVBT
members.

## Remove a committee

1. Select **Remove** beside the committee the person is leaving.
2. Choose the reason and select **Remove from committee**.
3. Confirm it is absent from the current list. **Past committees** retains the dates and reason.

Other committees and organization roles stay assigned. Google and Discord access follows the updated
committee assignments automatically; check **Accounts** if the person needs help with access.

## Give committee-lead access

Use this only when the person should help manage that particular committee in the staff portal.

1. Open **Committee lead access** below the committee list.
2. Find the relevant committee and choose **Committee lead access** or **Standard access**.
3. Select **Save access** for that committee.

The setting applies only to the named committee. It does not create an organization role or give
administrator access. Staff only see controls for committees they are allowed to manage.

If a form expires, reopen the profile and check the current list before trying again. If access is
denied, ask an administrator to review your permission; do not change another committee as a
workaround.
