# Welcome a new member

This guide explains how staff and committee leads personally welcome new LVBT members. Use the
welcome queue to avoid duplicate outreach and help each person find a useful next step.

## Before you start

- Sign in with your linked LVBT Workspace account and current membership. Staff administrators can
  see everyone waiting; committee leads see people whose selected interests match their committee.
- Choose the intended environment. Use test people in the protected preview and real members only in
  the approved live portal.
- Have an appropriate personal greeting ready. The portal records your outreach; it does not send a
  message for you or subscribe someone to another service.

## Choose and claim a person

1. Open **Welcome**. The queue shows current members who joined in the last 60 days and have not
   been welcomed, with those waiting longest first. **Waiting over a week** identifies people who
   have waited more than seven days.
2. Read their signup source, selected interests, and waiting time. The list omits email addresses
   and phone numbers. Another staff member's name means that person has already claimed the welcome.
3. Open the person and select **Assign to me** before contacting them. Contact details appear only
   to the current claimant. If someone else claimed them first, return to the queue and choose
   someone else.

Claims expire after seven days. Select **Unassign me** if you cannot follow through, so someone else
can help sooner. A claim reserves this outreach; it does not change membership or committee
assignments.

## Contact them and record the welcome

1. Choose an appropriate contact method. The email, phone, and **Send text message** links open your
   device's corresponding app. Review and send the message there yourself. A device without a text
   messaging app may not handle the text link; use another available method instead.
2. Introduce LVBT, acknowledge the interest they selected, and offer a concrete next step. Do not
   assume that they want to join every committee or use Discord.
3. After making contact, select the **Contact method** you actually used: email, phone, **Text
   message**, in person, or Discord. Add a short useful note if needed, without unnecessary personal
   details or copied message transcripts.
4. Select **Mark welcomed**. The person leaves the queue and the recorded welcome identifies you,
   the method, time, and any note. If saving fails, keep the claim and retry rather than sending
   another greeting.

Opening a contact app alone does not mark someone welcomed. Releasing a claim also does not record a
welcome. Committee leads receive only the contact details needed for the person they claimed; this
does not grant access to the full member roster.

## If something is missing

- **The queue is empty:** no eligible members are waiting within your scope. It does not mean the
  organization has no members.
- **A person disappears:** they may have been welcomed, left LVBT, passed the 60-day window, or
  moved outside your committee's selected interests. Return to the queue rather than bypassing the
  current access check.
- **Your claim expired or someone else holds it:** claim an available person again before opening
  contact details. Do not contact someone using an old saved page.
- **There is no usable contact method:** release the claim or ask an administrator for help through
  the usual private channel. Do not invent an email address or phone number.

## Production transition from the old welcome process

The protected preview does not retire the existing Google Form or Notion workflow. An operator must
complete the approved production cutover, verify a real form submission reaches the member records
without creating a new Notion welcome page, and give staff a retirement note before relying solely
on this queue. Keep the old Notion records available as read-only history; do not delete them as
part of this transition. See [Connect the membership form](./connect-the-membership-form.md) for the
existing form connection and [staff portal acceptance](../reference/staff-portal-acceptance.md) for
the launch checks.
