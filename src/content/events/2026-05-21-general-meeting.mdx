Our first general meeting. Drop in to meet the founders, hear the case for organized transit advocacy in the Valley, and figure out where you might plug in.

Sixty minutes. Cameras encouraged but optional. The shape:

- **10 minutes** — Who we are, why LVBT exists, and what we've already started.
- **20 minutes** — The Valley's transit moment in plain language: the bus network we have, what's about to be cut, and what the 2027 Nevada legislative session means for it.
- **15 minutes** — The five working initiatives — public education, coalition building, legislative advocacy, research and accountability, youth engagement — and where each one needs people.
- **15 minutes** — Open questions, then a quick match between volunteers and the work that fits them.

You don't need prior transit knowledge to belong here. If you ride the bus, drive in traffic, walk a sidewalk, or care about how Las Vegas grows over the next decade, this meeting is for you.

The Google Meet link works from phones and laptops — no account needed. We'll record audio for anyone who can't make it live, and post a written summary to the newsletter the next morning.
