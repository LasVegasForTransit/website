For Las Vegas residents who've never gotten to ride a transit system that is frequent, reliable, and
easy to use, our transit and urbanist education programming connects better city design to more
pleasant places to live.

That starts with showing what car dependency costs people every day: longer trips, hotter walks,
more dangerous streets, higher household expenses, and fewer choices for people who cannot or do not
want to drive.

The other half is showing what better transit can make possible. A good bus network is not just a
different way to commute. It changes who can reach school, who can keep a job without a car payment,
which neighborhoods feel connected, and how much public life a city can support.

The goal is to help more residents:

- notice when street and transit decisions affect ordinary life
- talk about buses, streets, land use, and funding without needing insider language
- compare what exists now with better choices other cities already take for granted
- bring their own experiences into public meetings, neighborhood conversations, and campaigns
