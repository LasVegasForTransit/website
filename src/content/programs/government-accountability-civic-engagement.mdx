Major transit decisions happen in public, but most residents don't have an easy way into that process, and agencies don't always have an easy way to reach the people affected by it. This program is the bridge: we track what's moving, explain it in plain terms, and connect residents directly to the people making the call.

When a transit decision is moving, people should be able to understand:

- who is making the decision
- what choice is actually on the table
- who benefits or gets left out
- what they can do about it

That works in both directions. We do public records work, voter education, and help residents understand when and how to speak at a public hearing, and we work with people already inside those agencies who want residents at the table.
