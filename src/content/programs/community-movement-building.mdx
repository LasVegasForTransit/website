Transit is one of the ways people in this Valley actually connect with each other. This program builds real relationships among the riders, students, workers, and neighbors across Southern Nevada who care about better transit and better neighborhoods.

Some of that happens through partnerships with institutions and community groups. Some of it is simpler: regular ways for people to meet, learn from each other, and get better at organizing together over time. Along the way, that also builds people who remember how things got done before, and a wider network of people who already care about transit.

Over time, that's what makes it possible for people to show up together when a campaign or hearing comes up, already knowing and trusting each other.
