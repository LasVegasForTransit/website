## About the role

You'll organize and own LVBT's events — and help dream up what they should even be. Transit doesn't have to be a dry policy topic. Part of this job is finding fresh, sometimes unexpected ways to get people excited about it: a county commission turnout, a community walk audit of a dangerous intersection, the awards night for our Viva Vegas contest — and whatever you can invent that we haven't thought of yet. The events are smaller and higher-stakes than most (getting 30 people to a commission meeting can change a vote), and the format is wide open.

This is a strong fit for students and early-career folks looking to build real civic organizing experience. You'll work with the Events Manager, with room to take the lead on your own ideas as you find your footing.

## What you'll do

You'll take events from idea to follow-up, across a mix of settings:

- Dream up new ways to get people excited about transit, and try them — not every idea has to work the first time
- Plan and run events end to end — community meetings, hearing turnouts, walk audits, socials, and one-offs like the Viva Vegas awards night
- Own the logistics: venue, setup, RSVPs, and day-of execution
- Write the invites, reminders, and follow-ups that actually get people to show up
- Debrief after each event so the next one runs better

## What we're looking for

No event-planning background needed — but this is more than a logistics gig. You're creative and a little fearless about trying new formats, you take initiative, and you're comfortable owning something start to finish. You can keep a few events in different stages moving at once, and you write clearly enough to get people to actually show up. Figure on about 3 hours a week, more around a big event.

It's a volunteer role, and a strong first one — you'll learn how a nonprofit organizes from the inside, run real events with your name on them, and build a network in the local advocacy world.
