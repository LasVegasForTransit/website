## Overview

Vegas Walking Tours are guided public walks that make the Valley legible on foot. Each tour follows a real route: bus stop to bus stop, cul-de-sac to grocery store, Strip job to residential neighborhood, or corridor to corridor.

The tours are public education, field observation, and a low-pressure way for curious residents to spend time with LVBT.

## Motivation

It is hard to argue for transit and walkable neighborhoods in a city where many people experience the Valley through a windshield. From inside a car, distance, heat, missing shade, sidewalk gaps, and transit access can remain abstract.

Walking the route together helps people understand why a crossing feels hostile, why a bus stop location matters, and why land use and transit cannot be separated.

## Approach

LVBT will pick routes that prove a point, co-host tours with partners, keep group sizes manageable, and document each walk through photos, audio, and written observations.

Each tour will include an easy way to stay involved, such as newsletter signup and a follow-up within 48 hours.

## What people will see

Each tour will leave behind a route note, photo essay, or short writeup that captures what the group saw and why the route matters. Where useful, LVBT will also publish short reels or maps that let people understand the walk without attending.

The series will create a public calendar of walks, partner-hosted events, and a social on-ramp for people who are curious about transit but not ready for a hearing, committee meeting, or campaign assignment.
