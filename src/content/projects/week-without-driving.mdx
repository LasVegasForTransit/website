## Overview

[Week Without Driving](https://weekwithoutdriving.org/) is a national challenge run by America Walks. For one week, people who normally drive get around another way and see the city the way people who can't drive see it every day.

LVBT is joining for the first time in 2026. Our version is a social media challenge: take one or more trips without driving between October 1 and 8, post about it, and tag us. Every day someone posts earns an entry in a giveaway. The rules and first-trip tips are on [the participant page](/week-without-driving).

## Motivation

Car dependency is easy to ignore from the driver's seat. It becomes obvious the moment you try to get across the Valley without one: a bus that comes every half hour, a sidewalk that ends at a wall, a shopping center you can see but can't safely walk to.

We want as many people as possible to have that experience and share it. Their posts do two things at once. They show neighbors what the system asks of people who ride, walk, or roll, and they build the audience LVBT needs on Instagram and TikTok, where younger Las Vegans already are.

The project also gives LVBT a public event to invite partner organizations into, from agencies and departments to other nonprofits, and it can become an annual program LVBT owns.

## Approach

The challenge is designed to be as easy to join as possible. One trip counts. A first bus ride counts. We are not asking anyone to go a whole week without a car, though we will celebrate the people who do.

During the week, LVBT's media committee posts at least two first-party stories a day and shares participant posts. A volunteer monitors the Instagram account so every mention is tracked. After the week, the committee produces a recap video and a written recap, and this site keeps the archive.

We are asking the RTC and other local organizations to participate and to share their own content during the week.

## Activities

- A participant page with the dates, the giveaway rules, and tips for a first bus trip.
- Daily Instagram stories during the week, plus participant posts we share.
- A giveaway of a one-month RTC bus pass and LVBT stickers, drawn from daily post entries.
- A recap video and blog post published after the week ends.
