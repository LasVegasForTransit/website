## Overview

Transit Diaries publishes first-person accounts from Las Vegans about how they actually move through the Valley. Riders submit day-in-the-life stories, and LVBT edits, illustrates, and publishes them with the writer's byline.

The project gives riders the page, not just a quote inside someone else's story.

## Motivation

Most coverage of Las Vegas transit is written by people who do not ride it. Riders are often treated as subjects: quoted briefly, summarized from a distance, and rarely allowed to explain the full shape of their day.

LVBT needs a public record of lived transit experience: the line cook getting home from the Strip at two in the morning, the student planning around headways, the parent juggling errands, or the retiree who stopped driving and kept moving through the city.

## Approach

LVBT will collect submissions through a standing form, pay contributors a small honorarium for published diaries, and edit in real conversation with the writer.

When it helps the story, each diary will include a strong photo, a small map, and consent-based reuse in scrollytellers, testimony, newsletter features, or op-eds.

## What people will see

Transit Diaries will publish a credited archive of rider stories on the site and in the newsletter, with enough geographic coverage to show how transit shapes daily life across the Valley rather than in one familiar corridor.

The archive will give LVBT primary-source material for public education, testimony, reports, and press outreach. When a story is reused, it will keep the rider's voice visible instead of turning their experience into anonymous evidence.
