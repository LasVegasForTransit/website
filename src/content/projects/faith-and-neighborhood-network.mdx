## Why this project

Elected officials hear advocacy organizations all day. What gets their attention differently is a pastor, an HOA board, a MADD chapter chair, the rabbi at the synagogue on Sahara, the imam at the mosque on Maryland — civic voices that don't come labeled "advocacy."

The Faith & Neighborhood Network builds those relationships before LVBT needs anything. It maps the religious communities and neighborhood associations along the Valley's transit-critical corridors, opens conversations on shared interests (kids walking to services, seniors who stopped driving, drunk driving, traffic deaths), and stays in touch.

## Where we're starting

- **Maryland Parkway corridor** — congregations and HOAs near the BRT alignment, especially in the corridor between Sahara and Tropicana
- **Charleston corridor** — communities along the active light-rail-vs.-BRT study area
- **Senior communities Valley-wide** — Sun City Summerlin, Sun City Anthem, Solera, Siena
- **MADD Southern Nevada** — Vegas is the third-most-deadly US city for pedestrians; this is their fight too

## Approach

- Map first — who's where, leadership names, regular meeting times, public stances on civic issues
- Lead with their concern, not transit — pedestrian safety near a school, a senior shuttle, a Vision Zero corridor next to a sanctuary
- Show up to their events before asking for anything; LVBT's name attached to a community day, a memorial walk, a back-to-school drive
- Trade — they get research, fact sheets, and visible LVBT support; LVBT earns the right to ask for theirs
