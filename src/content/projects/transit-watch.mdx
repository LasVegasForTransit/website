## Why this project

"I support transit" is a sentence anyone can say. Voting for it is not.

Transit Watch tracks the votes — who showed up, who voted yes, who voted no, who didn't vote — across every body whose decisions affect Las Vegas transit. The scorecard is the thing that makes the rhetoric and the record line up. It is not a hit list and it is not an endorsement; it is the public record, organized.

## Approach

- Define a public methodology — which votes count, how each is weighted, why
- Cite every scored vote with the agenda item, the date, and the official record
- Publish scorecards in plain language and as downloadable data
- Update on a public cadence so officials can see their own scores climb or fall in real time
- Coordinate scoring with the Local Government Program's meeting coverage and the State of Vegas Transit annual report
