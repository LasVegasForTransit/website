Email us with questions, tips, or stories about transit in Las Vegas. We respond to everything.

If you've got a story — a broken stop, a route you rely on, something wrong or something working — that's exactly what we want to hear. The work is built on those.

We're volunteers, so response times vary. But if you're on a deadline, say so.
